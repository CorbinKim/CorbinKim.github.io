# 1. Research Question
How can outliers be detected without labeled training data or tuned parameters, while still remaining fast and interpretable?

# 2. Related Works

### 2.1 Covariance based models
  - Minimum Covariance Determinant (MCD) 
### 2.2 Proximity based models
  - k-Nearest Neighbors Detector (kNN)
### 2.3 Linear based models 
  - One-class SVM detector
### 2.4 Ensemble based models
  - Locally Selective Combination of Parallel Outlier Ensembles (LSCP)
### 2.5 Neural network based models
  - Single-Objective Generative Adversarial Active Learning (SO-GAAL)
  - Multi-Objective Generative Adversarial Active Learning (MO-GAAL)

## Limitations of existing models
1. Their performances suffer from the curse of dimensionality, and are constrained to low-dimensional data.
2. Most methods require hyper parameter selection and tuning.


# 3. Contribution

1. Propose a novel, parameter-free outlier detection algorithm with both great performance and interpretability.
2. Perform extensive experiments on 30 benchmark datasets to show that COPOD outperforms in most cases and is also one of the fastest algorithms.
3. Release an easy-to-use Python implementation for reproducibility.
